# Launch screen

Use `UILaunchScreen` in Info.plist:

```xml
<key>UILaunchScreen</key>
<dict>
  <key>UIImageName</key><string>Brand/BrandMark</string>
  <key>UIColorName</key><string>Brand/IconBackground</string>
</dict>
```

The mark renders at its intrinsic 64 pt; dark appearance switches to the on-dark mark and `#15132E` automatically.
